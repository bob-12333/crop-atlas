Sources & how this works

NASA POWER provides climate averages. Crop reference ranges come from FAO EcoCrop through the OpenCLIM database mirror.

The comparison checks temperature and annual precipitation only. It does not measure your field’s soil or predict harvests. Seasonal rainfall, irrigation, extreme weather, and crop variety can change the outcome.

The month selector changes temperature only. Annual rainfall stays annual. Climate data represents an area around the point, not an individual field.

Satellite imagery and terrain: Esri and its contributors. Location search: Open-Meteo / GeoNames. Reference photographs: Wikimedia Commons, credited individually.
